PHINDA

The 40-year story of a land once lost, a wilderness forgotten, and the people who chose to bring it back.

About the Film

For forty years, a quiet experiment has been unfolding in South Africa.

Once a landscape emptied of much of its wildlife, Phinda has become one of the world’s most successful examples of large-scale ecological restoration. Rhinos, elephants, cheetahs and countless other species have returned, while neighbouring communities have become central to shaping its future.

PHINDA tells this remarkable story through the voices of the people who lived it.

Combining intimate personal testimony with four decades of archive and breathtaking wildlife cinematography, the film explores what happens when people choose to restore nature rather than exploit it.

At a time when environmental stories are often dominated by loss, PHINDA offers something increasingly rare: evidence that recovery is possible.

About the Filmmaker

WILL CLOTHIER is a UK-based documentary filmmaker specialising in conservation and character-led storytelling. His work explores the relationship between people and the natural world, with a particular focus on southern Africa and stories of ecological restoration, coexistence, and cultural connection to landscape.

His work is driven by a belief that conservation stories are ultimately about people - and that hopeful, emotionally grounded storytelling can help reconnect audiences with the living world.
